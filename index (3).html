On November 5, 160 vaccine professionals from 31 countries visit IVI on their GTH-B excursion. Over lunch, TCS is looking for 12 staff to each welcome one guest as a Secret Host.
The mission

So… what’s a Secret Host?

No agenda. No prep. Just a good conversation. We pair you with one GTH-B trainee in advance — all you do is show up. Enjoy a relaxed lunch and good conversation, one professional to another.
